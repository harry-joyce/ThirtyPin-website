Changelog

What’s new in ThirtyPin

A history of releases, fixes, and improvements. Older entries at the bottom.

v0.1.0 Coming Soon
Beta

First Public Release

New

  • Native macOS app built with SwiftUI, supporting macOS 13 Ventura and later.
  • Three-pane layout: sidebar, music library browser, device detail and sync controls.
  • Music library scanner that reads folder trees, metadata, embedded artwork, and folder-level art.
  • Sync planning — a full diff of source vs. device before any files move.
  • Two sync modes: Safe (only removes tracks ThirtyPin added) and Full Replace.
  • Automatic format transcoding via FFmpeg for incompatible audio formats.
  • Album artwork sync on all compatible iPod models.
  • Support for all iPod generations: Classic (1G–7G), Nano (1G–7G), Shuffle (1G–4G), mini.
  • Persistent selection memory per device — ThirtyPin remembers your choices across launches.
  • SQLite-backed settings, per-device track records, and conversion cache.

Known Limitations

  • This is an early release. Some edge cases around artwork handling on older devices are still being ironed out.
  • iPod Touch and iPhone are not supported; ThirtyPin targets the 30-pin connector era.
ThirtyPin

A modern macOS app for syncing music to classic iPods. Named after the connector that defined an era.
